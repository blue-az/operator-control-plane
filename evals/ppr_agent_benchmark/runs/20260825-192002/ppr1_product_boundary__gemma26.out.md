**SURFACES**
Registry tools, CLI `./ppr`, Charter/PBC, Analyst Desk.

**DATA_BOUNDARY**
Historical registry of 3,576 device models (2008–2025) for MDT, ABT, and BSX. Analyst Desk provides a published subset of 1,483 devices (41.5% of full).

**NON_GOALS**
Not clinical monitoring or device programming. Not a chatbot (queries use deterministic plan-building/tool dispatch). PDF extraction is offline ingestion, not part of the live query path.

**NUMBERS**
15 tools; 3,576 total models; 1,483 published models; 41.5% published coverage.

**RISK_NOTES**
Standalone extraction of PPR_Agent CRM domain; does not include the full Phoenix system.
