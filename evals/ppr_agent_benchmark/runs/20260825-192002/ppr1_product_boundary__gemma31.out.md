**SURFACES**
Registry tools, CLI `./ppr`, Charter/PBC, and Analyst Desk.

**DATA_BOUNDARY**
Analytical historical registry only. Data is divided between the full registry (3,576 device models) and the Analyst Desk published subset (1,483 devices), with the published subset comprising 41.5% of the full set.

**NON_GOALS**
No clinical monitoring or device programming. This is not a chatbot or free-text LLM assistant; it performs deterministic plan-building and tool dispatch.

**NUMBERS**
15 statically registered tools; 3,576 full devices; 1,483 published devices (41.5%).

**RISK_NOTES**
PDF ingestion is an offline process and is not part of the live query path.
