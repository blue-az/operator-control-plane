**SURFACES**
Registry tools, CLI `./ppr`, Charter/PBC, and Analyst Desk.

**DATA_BOUNDARY**
Analytical historical registry only. PDF extraction is handled via offline ingestion and is not part of the live query path.

**NON_GOALS**
Not for clinical monitoring or device programming. Not a chatbot or free-text LLM assistant; system uses deterministic plan-building and tool dispatch leveraging 15 statically registered tools.

**NUMBERS**
Full registry comprises 3,576 device models, while the Analyst Desk uses a published subset of 1,483 devices (41.5% of full).

**RISK_NOTES**
Missing charter fails closed.
