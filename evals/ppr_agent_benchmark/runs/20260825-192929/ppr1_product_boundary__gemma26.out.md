**SURFACES**
Registry tools, CLI `./ppr`, Charter/PBC, and Analyst Desk.

**DATA_BOUNDARY**
Historical registry (2008–2025) via offline PDF ingestion. Full registry of 3,576 device models vs. a published subset of 1,483 devices (41.5% of full).

**NON_GOALS**
Not clinical monitoring or device programming. Not a chatbot (utilizes deterministic plan-building and tool dispatch). Not the full Phoenix system.

**NUMBERS**
15 registered tools; 3,576 total devices; 1,483 published devices; 41.5% coverage; 92,071,191 total implants across 3 companies.

**RISK_NOTES**
Missing charter fails closed; partial charter parsing is fail-open. Queries for years outside 2008–2025 trigger warnings or capping.
