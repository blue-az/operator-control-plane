carrying its source URL. That is your only evidence. Do not browse or use outside sources.

You are reading as an informed outsider: a technical person who has never heard of this
system and is trying to work out what it actually is. Write your answer to `./ANSWER.md` —
ANSWER.md in your current working directory, the only place you may write.

## 1. What is it
Describe the system mechanically. What does it do, what are its parts, and how do they fit
together? Name the specific machinery, not the marketing framing. If the material uses a
term of art, say what the material says it means.

## 2. Demonstrated, or asserted
Separate what the material shows evidence for from what it states without support. Quote
the passage in each case. Where a page reports a result, say whether it also says how the
result was produced. Be exact: "verified" in a caption is not the same as a described method.

## 3. What you cannot tell
List the things an outsider genuinely cannot determine from this material, and say for each
what would settle it. Do not pad this with generic unknowns that apply to any system.

## 4. The likeliest misreading
One thing a reasonable, technically literate reader would probably conclude from this
material that the material does not actually support. Quote what invites the misreading.

Constraints:
- Do not praise the system, and do not soften findings. The author wants the outsider's read,
  not encouragement.
- Do not speculate about the author.
- Where two pages disagree with each other, say so and quote both.
- 900-1300 words.
